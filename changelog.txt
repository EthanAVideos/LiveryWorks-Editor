March 21, 2026. 1.2.6 Build 1.
    Sadly due to major limitations, and hours of work arounds, the more compatibly version was scrapped.

    This model using Python, and librarys: OpenGL, PyQt5, Numpy, Pillow, and Trimesh.
    Supports 3D models in the formats of OBJ, STL, and PLY formats.
    Fixes major texture and UV mapping display issues.
    Can load multiple logos of the format PNG, JPG, and BMP.
    Non-destructive editing: Logos are visual overlays, not baked into original texture

    Intuitive controls: Mouse-driven camera and logo placement.
    Professional UI: Grouped panels with clear hierarchy.
